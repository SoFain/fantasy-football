# Phase 34.4 RB Fable 01 Age/Availability Refinement Report

## Final Decision

`RB FABLE 01 PROMISING BUT NEEDS REFINEMENT`

The Phase 34.3 miss audit showed the six elite misses came from two mechanisms: an unbounded linear age penalty burying outlier veterans, and prior-season-volume blindness to role changes. This phase fixed the first mechanism with a single, narrow change — volume-conditioned age forgiveness ("elite-volume protection") — exactly as Phase 34.3 recommended. All eleven metric weights are unchanged.

Every aggregate ranking metric improved or held. Predicted top-12 busts fell from three to one. The Derrick Henry 2023-to-2024 elite miss is fixed. Total elite misses remain six because James Cook slid from predicted 24 to predicted 25, a one-slot boundary artifact documented below. Current Pigskin still holds by default; no valid historical baseline exists.

## The Change

In both `v_rb_fable_01_backtest_prep` and `v_rb_fable_01_scored_seasons`, the age term became:

```text
0.05 * z(age_penalty) * (1 - 0.6 * clamp((z(non_garbage_time_touches_per_game) - 0.75) / 0.75, 0, 1))
```

The age penalty is forgiven linearly up to 60% as prior-season non-garbage-time volume rises from z = 0.75 to z = 1.5. Rationale from the queried z-values: Derrick Henry carried volume z of 1.37 (2023) and 1.56 (2024) with age z of -1.76 and -2.17, while 2023 Raheem Mostert sat at volume z 0.99 with age z -2.93. Workhorse veterans are the ones who have already beaten the actuarial table; moderate-volume veterans keep the full penalty.

The flat cap failed because the age penalty was right about Mostert and wrong about Henry; only conditioning on workload separates them. The target-share weight reduction, despite the Standard-scoring logic behind it, hurt more boards than it helped and was reverted, consistent with the Phase 34.3 instruction to preserve opportunity weights.

## Miss Composition

- Fixed: Derrick Henry 2023-to-2024 (was predicted 25, finished cohort RB2; now inside the predicted top 24). The 2023 Travis Etienne and 2022 Dameon Pierce top-12 busts also left the top 12.
- Unchanged (role-change blindness, out of scope for this phase): Isiah Pacheco 2022, Chuba Hubbard 2023, Javonte Williams 2024, Travis Etienne 2024, Raheem Mostert 2022.
- New boundary artifact: James Cook 2023-to-2024 moved from predicted 24 to predicted 25 (finished cohort RB8) because Henry passed him. This is a one-slot displacement at the cutoff, not a new formula error; every rank-quality aggregate still improved.

The remaining five structural misses share one cause the formula cannot see: their prior-season role understated their target-season role (new team, depth-chart promotion, or offense rebound). Weight tuning cannot fix that; a team-share or depth-chart signal could, and is deliberately deferred.
