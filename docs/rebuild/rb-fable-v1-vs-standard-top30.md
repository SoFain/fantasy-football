This board is built from **2025 source metrics** and compared against the live 2026 preseason Current Pigskin Standard RB board (`pigskin-llm-20260704071412`, generated 2026-07-04). This is an owner-review comparison, not a backtest and not a live ranking.

## The Headline

The two boards mostly agree. 27 of 30 players appear on both lists, the top five are identical (McCaffrey, then Taylor/Robinson/Gibbs in slightly different order, Achane fifth on both), and most players sit within a few slots of each other. The value of the comparison is in the handful of principled disagreements.

## The Biggest Disagreements

**Derrick Henry (Fable 8, Standard 20).** The only ten-plus-spot gap on either board, and it is a direct product of the Phase 34.4 refinement: Henry's 18.6 non-garbage-time touches and 3.82 red-zone touches per game qualify him for elite-volume protection, so his age-31 penalty (-6, the largest on the board) is 56% forgiven. This exact mechanism fixed his 2023-to-2024 backtest miss, when the unprotected formula ranked him 25th and he finished RB2. The Standard board is making the same age bet the old formula made. This is the single best talking point of the comparison.

**Breece Hall (Standard 16, Fable 22).** Fable's discount is source-backed and specific: 1.63 red-zone touches per game and a 0.270 blended TD rate — bottom-quartile scoring opportunity for a back with his overall volume. In Standard scoring, where receptions earn nothing, that matters more than his name value.

**Zach Charbonnet (Fable 21, Standard 30).** The mirror image: modest overall volume but a top-ten red-zone role (3.38 red-zone touches per game). Fable's TD-centric Standard lens likes him; the Standard board sees a committee back.

**Aaron Jones and Tony Pollard (Standard 26/27, Fable 34/35).** Fable drops both veterans out of the top 30. Jones carries an age penalty of -5 with volume too low (13.3 touches/gm) to earn protection, plus a 0.71 availability rate. This is the age penalty working as intended on moderate-volume veterans — the same logic that correctly faded Raheem Mostert in the backtests.

## Known Weak Spots (say these out loud)

- **Four stale-team rows.** Etienne (now NO), Dowdle (now PIT), Walker (now KC), and Gainwell (now TB) are scored on last season's team context. Fable is structurally blind to role changes — the same blindness behind five of its six historical backtest misses.
- **James Conner is unrankable.** He has no qualified 2025 metric row, so Fable simply cannot see the Standard board's RB29.
- **Rookie efficiency reads are one season deep.** Jeanty's ugly efficiency line (-0.200 EPA/touch) may say more about the 2025 Raiders than about Jeanty.

## Recommendation

Keep RB Fable v1 **owner-review only** and let Current Pigskin hold live. The formula has earned a seat at the table — its disagreements are principled, source-backed, and in Henry's case validated by backtest evidence — but it has never beaten a historical baseline (none exists to test against) and it cannot see current depth charts. Next step: add a current-team context layer as review evidence, then decide whether the Henry/Hall/Charbonnet class of disagreements should influence the live Standard board.
